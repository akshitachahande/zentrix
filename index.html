About Zentrix Infotech

We are Zentrix Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

Zentrix (Avertis Infotech) commenced its operations as an application development company providing a highly specialized platform used to integrate with high-tech healthcare machinery. In today's evolving business landscape, we have come a long way and spread our expertise across diverse global industries.

We are a customer-focused and technology-driven software engineering and enterprise consulting company. We provide product engineering and enterprise software / application development services that help clients in crafting holistic value for their software development efforts. We understand our responsibility towards IT for our clients' business and shall always be committed to new changes by harnessing the power of technology to build effective business solutions.

Our Vision
